Privacy Policy

Air Traffic Control

Effective Date: October 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile game Air Traffic Control, developed by Jack Davies. I believe that your data is exactly that—yours. This game was built to provide a classic, uninterrupted gaming experience without harvesting user information.

1. Data Collection

Air Traffic Control does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data, device identifiers, or gameplay metrics. Everything you do in the game stays locally on your device.

2. Offline Operation

The game operates entirely offline. It does not require an internet connection to function, and it does not communicate with external servers in the background.

3. Advertisements & Tracking

Air Traffic Control contains no third-party advertisements, no microtransactions, and no tracking analytics. You will never be targeted by ads or data brokers through this application.

4. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this privacy policy or the game itself, please feel free to reach out via the Contact Page on my portfolio.
