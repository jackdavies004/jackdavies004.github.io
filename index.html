Dissertation: A Game of Trust

For my third year dissertation project I was tasked with building a live-service multiplayer game that would quantify trust levels for each of the respective players. It encouraged players to play from each others perspectives and required teamwork and deception to win. I coded the client and server side in Java, and used an AWS EC2 instance to facilitate the multiplayer hosting. I received a 2:1 for my project's final result.
